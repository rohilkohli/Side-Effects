        Why you can trust the warnings

        136,270real US consumer complaints, read with Gemini and scored with the statistic drug regulators use.
        2.1×Apple Card dispute complaints vs similar card issuers: the problem the US regulator fined Goldman Sachs $45M for, found from public data alone.
        3.0×complaints per card at Indian small finance banks vs the national average (RBI Ombudsman, 2024-25).
        19%of complaints in a checked sample were copy-paste templates. Gemini filters them out so they can't fake a warning.
        100%of contract quotes are checked word for word against the contract. Anything that can't be found is thrown away.
        0numbers made up by AI. Every figure is computed from the data; Gemini reads, code counts.

        Methodology

        PRR = a / (a + b)c / (c + d)

        a complaints about this problem at this lender, b its other complaints, c and d the same for similar lenders. A signal needs PRR ≥ 2, at least 10 complaints and a Yates chi-squared ≥ 4, the same rule drug regulators use for adverse-event reports.

        Template filtering. Gemini flags complaints that are copy-paste templates (19% of a checked sample), so a campaign of identical letters cannot fake a warning.

        Safety alerts compare the last 45 days with the 180 before, with the same thresholds. Code does every count; Gemini only writes the words.
