The problem

People sign up for credit with no warning label, and soon AI agents will sign for them.

The advert shows the upside

"0% interest", "pay in 4", "no hidden fees". What goes wrong for real customers is never on the page.

The evidence already exists

Regulators publish hundreds of thousands of consumer complaints. Nobody turns them into a warning you see at the moment you sign.

Agents make it faster

Shopping agents and agent payment protocols are arriving. An agent told to "keep the monthly cost low" will pick the trap in milliseconds.

Medicine solved this decades ago: every packet carries a leaflet, and regulators mine adverse-event reports for signals. Finance has the same raw material and no leaflet.

The insight

Run drug-safety statistics on financial complaints.

  • 136,270 real US CFPB complaints about cards, personal loans and BNPL.
  • Gemini codes each story into 26 financial "side effects" and filters copy-paste templates (19% of a checked sample).
  • Proportional reporting ratio with a 95% interval and the Evans rule: the same signal test drug regulators use.
  • The leaflet says "reported 2.1x more often than similar lenders", never "common" or "rare". Complaints are unverified, and the leaflet says so.

Does it find real problems?

2.1×

Apple Card (Goldman Sachs): purchase disputes left unresolved, vs peer issuers. 817 complaints in 2024, still 2.1x in Jun-Sep 2026.

The US CFPB ordered Goldman Sachs to pay $45M over exactly this in October 2024. Our method finds it from public complaints alone.

For people: the leaflet

Photograph any offer. Get the leaflet it should have come with.

  • Each line says what it is: a bank-level signal, a product-category signal, or a clause in this contract.
  • Gemini reads every page of the contract and pins the clause behind each side effect; code checks the quote word for word.
  • "Before you sign" warnings for your situation, interactions, and lenders without these signals ("consider a generic").
  • A pharmacist you can ask by voice, answering only from the leaflet, the contract and real complaints.

How it works

Five Gemini agents around a deterministic core.

Intake agentGemini, multimodal: photo, PDF or text of an offer → product, lender, what the advert leaves unsaid
Signal engineCode: PRR vs peer lenders over 136k complaints, plus RBI rates for India. Every number comes from here.
Clause readerGemini, long context, structured output: pins each side effect to a clause → code verifies every quote
Leaflet writerGemini: contraindications and interactions for this person, citing only line ids it was given
PharmacistGemini with tools: get_side_effects, search_contract, complaint_examples, compare_with
Shopping agentGemini function calling, run step by step; thoughts and tool calls stream to the page
Agent gate APIPOST /api/v1/check → no_signal / disclose / human_review. No model in the loop: same request, same answer.

Blue = Gemini. Green = deterministic code. Offline, BigQuery runs Gemini over complaint stories and computes the statistics.

Why Gen AI, and how we keep it honest

Gemini does the reading. Code does the counting.

Only Gen AI can

  • Read a photo of a flyer and a 40-page contract in any layout
  • Turn free-text complaint stories into a side-effect vocabulary, and spot templates
  • Explain a clause in plain words for this person's situation
  • Act as an agent: plan, call tools, stop and ask

Guardrails in code

  • No number ever comes from the model
  • Clause quotes verified word for word; unfound quotes discarded
  • Warnings must cite a real side-effect line, or they are dropped
  • The gate lives in the checkout, not the prompt
  • No API keys: Vertex AI via the Cloud Run service identity

Who it helps

One evidence base, three ways in.

People

A leaflet for any card, BNPL plan or loan, written for their situation, before they sign. Free, in the browser, from a photo.

AI agent builders

One API call before an agent commits a person to credit. A machine-readable verdict and the disclosures to show. The safety layer agentic commerce is missing.

Banks and regulators

The same signals are an early warning: the Apple Card dispute problem was visible in public complaints. Lenders can fix a side effect before it becomes an enforcement action.

Freelancers, gig workers and people already juggling pay-later plans get hurt most by repayment and interest surprises. They are who the "Before you sign" warnings are written for.
